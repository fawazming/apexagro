| ---------------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Deep Forest Green**  | `#0B5E2A` | Primary brand color, logo, headings |

| **Fresh Leaf Green**   | `#8DC63F` | Highlights, growth accents          |
| **Harvest Gold**       | `#F4C430` | Logo lettering, CTAs, emphasis      |

### 4. Typography

**Primary Typeface — Poppins**

Recommended weights:

* Poppins Bold — major headings
* Poppins SemiBold — subheadings
* Poppins Medium — buttons and labels
* Poppins Regular — supporting text

**Secondary Typeface — Lato**

Recommended weights:

* Lato Bold — secondary headings
* Lato Regular — body copy
* Lato Light — captions/supporting information

**Typography hierarchy**

```text
APEX GLOBATECH AGRO
Poppins Bold

Section Heading
Poppins SemiBold

Supporting Heading
Lato Bold

Body Copy
Lato Regular

Caption / Metadata
Lato Regular
```

### 7. Brand Pattern

**Pattern concept:**
A subtle repeating pattern of leaves, seedlings and organic botanical forms.

**Recommended treatment:**

* Use `#8DC63F` or `#1D7A34`
* 5–12% opacity
* Keep pattern subtle
* Avoid competing with text or logo
* Suitable for packaging, letterheads, social graphics and backgrounds
