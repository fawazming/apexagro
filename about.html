About Apex Globatech Agro

Growing Agriculture. Empowering Farmers. Your trusted partner for agricultural growth.

Our Story

Building a Better Agricultural Future

Apex Globatech Agro Ltd is an agricultural company based in Remo Sagamu, Ogun State, Nigeria. We are committed to helping farmers achieve higher productivity through quality planting materials, reliable agricultural services and expert guidance.

We supply quality pineapple suckers, oil palm seedlings, plantain and banana suckers, cocoa seedlings, coconut seedlings and other farm planting materials — while providing farm establishment, agricultural consultation and agro-support services.

We are committed to honesty, quality and customer satisfaction while building lasting partnerships with farmers and agricultural businesses.
