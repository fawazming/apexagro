Who We Are

Growing Agriculture. Empowering Farmers.

Apex Globatech Agro Ltd is an agricultural company based in Remo Sagamu, Ogun State, Nigeria. We supply quality pineapple suckers, oil palm seedlings, plantain and banana suckers, cocoa seedlings, coconut seedlings and other farm planting materials.

Beyond planting materials, we provide farm establishment, agricultural consultation and agro-support services — helping farmers build sustainable, productive agricultural businesses.
